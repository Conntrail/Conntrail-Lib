Key Takeaways:
- Clear, specific queries have LOW entropy (stable decisions)
- Vague, ambiguous queries have HIGH entropy (fragile decisions)
- The on_alert callback can notify you when models are uncertain
- Use export_format='jsonl' for audit trails and persistence
